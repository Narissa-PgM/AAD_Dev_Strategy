Operating model · 1 · Coordination

One Harvard

Federated data for a decentralized University, and leadership without formal authority. Harvard’s Schools and units own their data, relationships and decisions; the Director connects them through shared definitions and one calendar, so leadership sees one University-wide picture without overriding anyone.

Operating model · 2 · Donor-centred growth

Impact Circle

The donor at the centre, with every School and unit in the circle. Each School keeps its own records and donor relationships, and federated data gives everyone one view of the same donor. Automation drafts each next step, and a named person approves it before anything reaches a donor. Stewardship is pipeline: each report opens the next conversation.

Action plans for the four measures most at risk

From the risks on page 03, with what I would do before each happens, the early warning, and the response agreed in advance.

A03 · Jan 8

Council charter stalls on a School disagreement

Why it is at risk

The charter needs every School to accept shared definitions. One objection can hold the dictionary and the pilot.

What I would do now

  1. Sign with the two willing Schools first, so the work starts on Jan 8.
  2. Put disputes to the council with a dispute route in the charter, and record dissent.
  3. State in the charter that no authority over Schools is implied.

Early warning

A School asks for more time twice, or a draft returns with new conditions.

If it happens

Proceed with the willing Schools and keep the seat open. The council takes the dispute at its next meeting.

Owner · by when

AVP, with the convenor · Jan 8

What it protects

Gate 1 stays on Jan 29, and the dictionary on Feb 26.

A04 · Jan 15

Delivery capacity is not protected

Why it is at risk

Two FTE is a planning figure. Without named people and hours, the build competes with daily reporting and slips.

What I would do now

  1. Name the people and the hours, and cost the backfill, by Jan 15.
  2. Start a time log in December, so capacity is measured, not assumed.
  3. Agree the scope rule now: what is cut first if capacity falls short.

Early warning

Logged hours fall below plan for two weeks running.

If it happens

Gate 1 reduces scope to the confirmed capacity. No automatic hiring and no contingency draw.

Owner · by when

AVP · Jan 15

What it protects

The $250K ceiling is spent on work that can finish.

A06 · A09 · Jan 29

Data access is slow

Why it is at risk

Source permissions and baseline extracts depend on data owners outside AA&D.

What I would do now

  1. Request source-specific permission in week 3, before the inventory starts.
  2. Plan with synthetic or approved aggregate data until access is granted.
  3. Hold any work on restricted data until its permission is signed.

Early warning

Any permission open for more than three weeks.

If it happens

Escalate to the AVP and the data owner, and freeze the baseline on the sources that are approved.

Owner · by when

Data owners, Data & HUIT · Jan 29

What it protects

Gate 1 evidence is complete on the approved sources.

A17 · Aug 31

Adoption stays below 80%

Why it is at risk

Logins are not adoption. If officers do not use the action queue, the benefit case fails.

What I would do now

  1. Measure adoption as real decision tasks completed, not logins.
  2. Fix the slowest journey with its users before each expansion wave.
  3. Retire the duplicate report as soon as the replacement is accepted.

Early warning

Adoption below 60% in any School after one month.

If it happens

Hold the next wave, retrain in that School, and redesign the task before Gate 3.

Owner · by when

Director, with School leads · Aug 31 (Gate 3)

What it protects

The benefit register at Gate 4 counts real use only.

The operating model applied

One HarvardShared definitions and controls; the Schools keep their decision rights and donor relationships. The coordinator has no implied authority over Schools.
Impact CircleI3 pilots the loop from listening and alignment through verification and communication to renewal. Each cycle has an accountable relationship owner, and every donor commitment is approved by a person.
SMARTERA baseline, then a bounded pilot, then evaluated expansion. Targets are fixed at Gate 1 and reviewed at every gate after it.
VERITASEvery figure traces to its source and agrees with Finance. Synthetic evidence never satisfies a control gate.
